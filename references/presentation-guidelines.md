## Verified January 2026 proposal language

Use these motifs when a digital surface or slide should feel continuous with the proposal:

- Large white field with a strong blue edge or panel.
- Oversized cropped `NYCU LIFE` typography.
- Low-opacity fox watermark.
- Thin vertical blue rule beside bilingual section titles.
- Rounded blue information cards with generous whitespace.
- Primarily blue and white composition; auxiliary blue only for hierarchy, not body-text contrast.

## Slide recommendations

- Keep one message per slide and one dominant visual anchor.
- Use the official outlined logo assets; do not recreate lockup text.
- Place the primary horizontal lockup in ordinary content slides and the vertical lockup on centered covers.
- Use Logo Blue `#0922E7` consistently for brand fields. Treat legacy proposal blues as visual references until the owner formally names them.
- Keep body text on white or Night Blue rather than on the proposal's mid-blue card when text is small.
- Maintain Traditional Chinese and English as deliberate pairs, not duplicate decoration.

## Before publishing a deck

Check edit rights, font availability, embedded media, logo clear space, text contrast, export page size, and PDF rendering. Preserve a local PDF reference for review, but do not bundle the source proposal into a distributable skill unless the owner confirms redistribution rights.
