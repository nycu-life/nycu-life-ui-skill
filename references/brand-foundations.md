# Brand foundations

## Brand idea

NYCU LIFE organizes fragmented campus information into a clear, warm entry point for student life.

The official mark combines four meanings:

- Fox: the NYCU bamboo fox connection, flexibility, intelligence, and companionship.
- Book: the lines above the ears suggest pages and gathered knowledge.
- C-shaped tail: the tail forms the `C` in `NYCU` and creates a memorable enclosure.
- Pencil tip: the tail ending implies turning information into action.

Use the working brand promise: **把校園生活，整理成一個好入口。**

## Personality

### Clear

Lead with the answer. Use visible hierarchy, concrete labels, and one primary action per decision point.

### Connected

Show relationships between information, services, places, time, and next steps. Avoid isolated modules with no path forward.

### Warm

Write like a capable student guide: friendly, calm, specific, and never bureaucratic or overly cute.

## Voice

- Prefer: `活動已加入行程，開始前 30 分鐘提醒你。`
- Avoid: `您的請求已成功被系統處理。`
- Prefer verbs that describe the next step: `查看路線`, `加入行程`, `重新整理`.
- Explain errors with cause and recovery. Do not blame the user.
- Keep bilingual labels short. Use Traditional Chinese as the primary campus language unless the product context says otherwise.

## Visual language

Use large blue fields, generous whitespace, cropped brand type, fine vertical rules, rounded information cards, and a restrained fox watermark. Keep the fox mark official; do not invent new poses or facial expressions.

Use Logo Blue `#0922E7` as a distinctive brand anchor. Use Action Blue and semantic product colors for interface work.

## Typography

The January 2026 proposal embeds PingFang TC, Noto Sans CJK SC, and Montserrat, but does not define a licensed cross-platform type system. Use:

- UI, headings, and long-form Traditional Chinese: Noto Sans TC first, then system sans and PingFang TC fallbacks.
- English inside the website uses the same Noto Sans TC stack for a quieter bilingual rhythm; reserve Montserrat only for separately approved campaign artwork.
- Logo text: always use the outlined official asset. Never typeset it manually.
